Klassik 0.10.2

Show taps for screen recordings
- Diagnostics > Recording > Show Taps: turn it on. A grey circle shows at each touch. sz-rs30.1.66
- Start a screen recording from Control Center. Tap, scroll and swipe. The recording shows each circle.
- Open a menu or a swipe action. The circles show there too.
- Close and open Klassik. The setting stays on.
- Turn it off. No more circles.

Known problems
- Circles do not show on Control Center, the lock screen or other apps.
