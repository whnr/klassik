Klassik 0.9.0

Sets and tags
- Composer page (try Chopin): works with a GROUPING tag sit in sets, such as "Nocturnes, Op. 9". Tap a set to open it. sz-rs30.1.51
- Sets sort in natural order: Op. 7 before Op. 17.
- Works without a GROUPING tag stay in the Works list.
- Recording > Tags and Grouping: shows the GROUPING tag. Genres show once. sz-rs30.1.52
- Open Tags from a whole-album play: the text says "A whole album played as one work". sz-rs30.1.52

Library and storage
- Library > Classical Albums: a tap opens the album. Play from the album page, or use the row menu. sz-rs30.1.47
- Home > Local Storage: the cache limit is a stepper in whole GB. It reads "2 GB", not "2.05 GB". sz-rs30.1.46

Known problems
- Sets do not play as a whole yet. Play a piece, or shuffle the composer.
- Sets do not remember if they are open.
