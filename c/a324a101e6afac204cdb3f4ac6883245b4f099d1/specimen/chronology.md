# Chronology

Rules for `chronology.html`. The terms **active years** and **contemporary** are in `CONTEXT.md`.

- Two levels: an overview of all composers, then one composer. The overview shows each composer as a line and the periods as bands. A tap on a line opens that composer.
- The Chronology opens on the composer of what plays, with that work marked. With nothing playing, it opens where the listener left it.
- Years run down the screen. The composer's life fits one screen.
- The works of the focus composer show as dots on the line and as rows on the right: year and title. When the rows do not fit, one row stands for a year or a span, with a count. Undated works are one row at the end of the line, with a count.
- A tap on a work opens its work page, so the work page says which recording plays. A tap on a counted row opens the works drawer. A tap on the composer's name opens the composer page.
- The line of a composer is bright from the start of their active years and dim before. Without a work period start, the line has no shading.
- Contemporaries are plain lines without a name, with a dot for each dated work in the library. A composer without life dates stays off the chart.
- A sideways drag scrubs across the composers on the screen. The width of the chart maps onto those lines in order. The label names the composer under the finger, and that line is highlighted. The chart stays still during the drag and glides to that composer on release. It never zooms.
- The playing composer takes the accent. The focus composer and the last opened composer take ink.
- The works drawer uses the plain work rows of a page, on the page ground at every detent.
