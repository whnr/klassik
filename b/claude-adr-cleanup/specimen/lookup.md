# MusicBrainz and Wikidata page

Rules for `lookup.html`. The lookup rules are in [ADR 0011](../../docs/adr/0011-enrichment.md) and [ADR 0042](../../docs/adr/0042-wikidata-pass-in-the-background.md).

- The page shows the progress, the counts, and the works to confirm or without a match.
- "Look Up Missing Again" asks again for every work without a match. A match that the listener turned down stays turned down.
